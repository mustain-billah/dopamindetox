Stay away from

  • YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn
  • Newspapers — online and print, sports pages included
  • Videos, films, reels and games — on any device
  • Any other social platform

Messaging apps

WhatsApp, Messenger and Telegram are a phone, not a feed. Talking to someone is what they are for, and that stays open to everyone.

  • Communication only — calls, voice notes, one-to-one and group chat, class and job notices, and anything a person sent you directly: someone's notes, a job circular, a work document. This is a clean day, and it needs no explanation.
  • Others — Status and Stories, Channels, and videos, reels or news links you opened because they were forwarded. A reel is a reel whether it came from Instagram or from your cousin. This counts as a slip.
  • No — you did not open a messaging app at all that day. Same as communication only for scoring; it is there so nobody has to claim they chatted when they didn't.

Messenger is counted here, not under Facebook. Facebook itself — the feed, Stories, Watch, Marketplace — is still out.

You can still

  • Open YouTube or LinkedIn for study or a job, or read news, articles and industry updates for work or official duty. Mark the day study or work and say what it was — that note is the evidence, and the form asks for it.
  • Get class notices in person or some other way.

Who wins

  • Whoever stays away for the whole time wins {{ currency }}{{ prize }}.
  • A study-or-work day does not put you out of the competition — you are still in it. But when we pick the winner, whoever used none of these at all comes first.
  • So the order is: fewest days you slipped, then fewest study-or-work days, then most days filled in.
  • You can take part even if you have no Android phone or laptop.

Who checks

Nobody. There is no way to see what anyone actually did — it rests on each person's own honesty. This page does not watch anyone. Everything on it was typed in by the person it belongs to, and only that person can change it.

One thing worth knowing: the people organising the challenge can open any participant's log and read what you wrote in what you did instead and reason. Everyone else only ever sees your totals on the board. Write accordingly.
